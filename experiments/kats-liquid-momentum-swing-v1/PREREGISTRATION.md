## Trading rules fixed before outcomes
At each completed trading session close, if flat and entry would be at least 20 trading sessions before partition end, rank the eight symbols. Need >=64 valid daily sessions, close > SMA20 > SMA60, 63-valid-day momentum (close / close from 63 sessions ago − 1) >= 5%, close at least 98% of preceding 63-session maximum close, previous 20-day average daily rupee turnover >= INR 2 crore, and ATR14 > 0. Score = 63-session momentum + 0.5 * 21-session momentum. Select greatest score, tie alphabetically. Only one position/pending entry; no future-return-based stock selection or skipped losers.

Next valid calendar session at 09:15, buy at that symbol's raw daily open plus frozen 5bps slippage (tick rounded up), provided opening price remains above previous-day structural stop. Initial stop = decision close − max(2 x ATR14, 0.02 x close), tick rounded down. No stop tightening for affordability. Whole shares; cash-funded, max 1% current realized equity **including cost and modeled stop slippage**, pick largest affordable quantity, no leverage. If none fits, no trade that day. Entry minute stop path only evaluates the same daily bar **after** opening price and never uses its high/low for selection.

Daily protective stop: if open <= current stop, sell at open less slippage; else if low <= stop, sell at stop less slippage, acknowledging daily bars do not prove actual stop order fill. Intraday stop has precedence over close-based exits. Trailing stop may be increased only **after session close** for the next trading session to max(existing, close − 2 x ATR14). Exit next session's 09:15 open upon completed close below prior 10-session SMA or after 15 observed holding sessions; exit at scheduled next open; no 15:20 exit. No overlap. Never treat carried-forward no-volume intraday bars as executable prices.
