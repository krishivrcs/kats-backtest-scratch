# KATS-INTRADAY-300NET-V1 — frozen 2026-10-10, pre-outcome

**Target:** INR 300 net **on successful trading days**, not INR 300 guaranteed each day. A day with no qualified trades must be NO_TRADE. We must not pretend that a target is a forecast of hitting it.

**Account:** INR 20,000 cash; no leverage, whole shares, maximum one intraday round-trip/day, <=INR 200 cost-inclusive **planned** stop-out loss per trade (<=1% of current equity, also capped at INR 200), no overnight positions. Stops do not cap gap/slippage losses. No live broker orders, no paper execution engine. Investor cannot be told profit is ensured.

**Universe and evidence:** frozen eight-symbol liquid-equity universe from existing KATS scratch loader: AXISBANK, DLF, HAL, ICICIBANK, INFY, KOTAKBANK, SBIN, TCS; downloaded public minute source with SHA256 `20024713c455cc16b5daae91e06991d57a1acfa6a30c77bb7d5a742ee1789ab2`. The source is research grade and includes corporate-action hazards, not exchange/order-book evidence. Exclude sessions failing source integrity; no interpolation. Observation uses completed five-minute bars and the next **one-minute bar + 1 minute** for entry. Never fill using 15:20 zero-volume carried-forward quotes. Candidate entry price uses a *future observed bar open* as simulation, not information allowed for signal formation.

**Partition:** development 2024-04-01 to 2025-02-28, warmup from 2024-02-01; only if development criteria pass, score validation 2025-03-01 to 2025-10-31. Original KATS sealed November 2025–April 2026 holdout untouched. Both partitions already encountered in other research → **not truly virgin OOS**. Reject when insufficient robust sample.

**Hypothesis (predeclared):** first-half intraday trend continuation after 45-minute 5m volatility/range break with 5m volume impulse and same-session VWAP confirmation. No EMA/RSI optimization. May be long OR short (short must be covered same day), only NSE cash-equity MIS, and no presumed margin leverage.
- Wait for 10:00–12:30 IST. On each completed 5min bar, require preceding 9 completed 5min bars. Long: current five-minute close >= previous 9-bar highest high * 1.0005; five-minute bar green, close > cumulative VWAP calculated at its end; current volume >= 1.3x median of prior nine bars, and opening 15-min relative volume >= 1.15x average prior 20 valid sessions. Mirror for short with low / red bar / close<VWAP.
- Signal is only valid after bar completes. Entry is at next 5-min bar's second 1-min bar open (sixth minute after signal bar start). Entry minute must have positive observed volume and valid prices.
- Stop anchored to last 3 completed 5-minute bars' extreme, offset by INR 0.05 tick (down for long, up for short). Reject any invalid entry/stop or zero-quantity risk.
- To prevent mathematically unreachable net profit targets, net-300 exit target must be no farther than 1.5 times **median of prior 20 valid trading-session daily high-low ranges**, known before trade day. Otherwise reject before seeing trade outcome. Historical range is NOT a guarantee future excursion.
- Long/short quantity chosen conservatively as largest whole quantity with entry-side costs and <= INR 20k notional (no margin). On planned stop-fill, total adverse P&L including entry/exit broker fees, STT, exchange fees, SEBI, IPFT, GST, stamp and modeled slippage <= min(INR 200, 1% current equity).
- Net-300 take profit is computed in 5-paise tick increments after quantity, fees and adverse slippage; skip trade if target/range filter fails. Take profit only where modeled cash P&L >= INR 300 after all costs. No minimum gross-profit proxy.
- Same-day 1m exit: stop first on ambiguous candle, then take-profit, else mandatory exit at 15:10 IST exact 1-minute bar OPEN with volume>0. Both stop and target price crossings require a positive-volume 1m bar. A missing executable observation is DATA_UNSCORABLE, never a free favorable fill. Forced exit at 15:10 cannot guarantee actual brokerage execution. No pyramiding, no re-entry, no overnight.
- Candidate priority: first chronologically eligible setup by entry timestamp, then descending volume ratio, then alphabetical symbol; one round trip/day. Filter all candidates on economic feasibility BEFORE selecting first eligible trade; compare within-day occupancy. Candidate sorting fixed before inspecting any result.

**Costs:** Upstox PLUS NSE equity intraday brokerage `min(INR 30, 0.1%*turnover)` per filled order; STT 0.025% of sell-side turnover; stamp 0.003% buy-side; SEBI INR10/crore; NSE trans fee 0.00322% before Oct 2024, 0.00297% Oct 2024–Feb 2026; IPFT 0.0001% both sides (conservative historic treatment), GST 18% brokerage+transaction+SEBI+IPFT. This is a *predeclared approximate historical charges model*, unverified against each contract note; date-specific broker terms and penny rounding may differ. Basic case slippage = 5bps per entry/exit, adverse = 15bps each side. Brokerage on short follows buy/sell turnover and no DP charge if fully intraday.

**Research pass:** Development >=35 closed trades, positive BASE net and adverse net, profit factor >1.15, max realized drawdown <=INR 3,000, >=50% profitable traded months, net profit excluding three biggest winners >0, and at least 12 modeled net-300 target wins. Validation if accessed: >=25 trades, BASE and adverse net>0, profit factor>1.1, max DD<=INR 3k, >=50% positive months, net ex top 3 >0, and at least 8 modeled +300 wins. A pass means PROMISING_REQUIRES_INDEPENDENT_EXECUTION_REVIEW, **not demonstrated executable profit or permission for live orders**. If fail, REJECT without tuning and leave validation untouched.

**Important:** No alpha verified before experiment; risk model is a planning cap not an ironclad loss limit. User's demand is INR 300 daily earnings: this model tests that feasibility transparently with ZERO no-signal days counted, rather than manufacturing a daily average.
