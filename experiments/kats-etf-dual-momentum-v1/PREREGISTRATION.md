# KATS-ETF-DUAL-MOMENTUM-V1 — fixed research protocol (2026-10-10)
## Goal and non-guarantee
Determine whether a low-turnover, cash-funded India-listed equity/gold ETF rotation rule has positive after-cost return on public NSE daily OHLCV, compared with NIFTYBEES/GOLDBEES buy-and-hold and no-trade. Research-only, no broker orders. Does not assert future profitability.
## Data
NSE-origin native unadjusted daily bhavcopy data for `NIFTYBEES` and `GOLDBEES` from the connected NSE daily-history source. Confirm no relevant splits/bonuses; no invented missing sessions. Use all common trading dates Jan 2022–Oct 2025 with warmup and enumerate excluded rows. No series from Nov 2025 onward; original KATS holdout sealed.
## Periods and testing
Warmup 2022; disclosed development Jan–Dec 2023; conditional validation Jan 2024–Oct 2025. Multiple prior KATS candidates studied overlapping months (acknowledge multiple testing); neither is truly fresh independent prospective proof. Return attribution per partition independent reset to ₹20,000.
## Frozen daily trading strategy
At each *completed month-end close*, for each ETF compute 126-common-trading-session price momentum = CLOSE(today) / CLOSE(today−126) − 1, and its rolling SMA200 inclusive of today's close. Eligible only if last CLOSE>SMA200 and momentum>0. Target the single eligible ETF with greater momentum (lexicographic tie to GOLDBEES); if none, 100% cash, 0% credited cash interest. Signals fixed at completed month-end close; trade at the OPEN of the NEXT common trading session. No intramonth trading. One holding at a time, whole shares, fully cash financed. Hold until next month-end target switch, then sell old asset at next open and buy new asset at same next open. If unchanged, do nothing. On final partition trading day open, force-liquidate as explicit research truncation, and prohibit new entries during this last day; mark any mismatch as unscorable.
## Execution / costs
For every modeled buy: open adverse 5bps, broker delivery ₹0, ETF stamp duty 0.015% on buy, exchange and SEBI turnover, GST on eligible fee categories. Sell open adverse 5bps, NSE delivery ETF STT 0.001% sell ONLY for NIFTYBEES; STT NIL for GOLDBEES, DP debit ₹15.34 per exit, plus exchange/SEBI/GST. Assume conservative NSE transaction charge 0.00307% each side, SEBI 0.0001% and GST 18% on exchange+SEBI, with disclosed research rounding limitations. Alternative adverse: 15bps one way, same underlying signals, transaction costs. Do not treat daily OHLC bars as actual broker fills; lack of spreads/available depth is an explicit blocker to real executability. Reject if daily volume<=0, OHLC bad, or adjusted price discontinuity unresolved.
## Decision
Do not tune thresholds or lookback after observing results. For a **promising** result require validation >=12 completed monthly intervals, positive net and positive adverse net, drawdown <=15%, and a positive return after expenses in at least half validation calendar years. Must also compare head-to-head to NIFTYBEES/GOLDBEES buy-and-hold benchmarks; clearly state if strategy underperforms. Any promising strategy still requires independent review and fresh forward paper trading to claim practical capability to earn. Validation fail => REJECT. No live execution authorized.
